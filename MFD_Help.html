How to use the MFD Records System
← Back to Hub

Topics

This guide covers everything in the MFD Records System. A Everyone tag means any signed-in member can do it. An Admin tag means it needs an Admin account. You can also print this page from your browser's print menu.

Getting started

Signing in

Enter your last name and your password. Admins create each member's login from the Roster (open a member, then the Login Access section). If you can't sign in or you forgot your password, ask an Admin to reset it.

The bar at the bottom of every page

  • Your name shows who is signed in and whether the account is Admin or Personnel.
  • 🔑 Change Password lets you set your own password.
  • 🚪 Log Out signs you out. Always log out on a shared computer or station tablet.
  • ❓ Help opens this guide.
  • 📋 Reports opens the list of reports members have submitted (reports are submitted from the Time Card page).

Phones, tablets and computers

Everything works on a computer or tablet. On a phone, the Roster shows a simple read-only directory with tap-to-call numbers. For full details and editing, use a computer or tablet.

Where your data is kept

Whatever you enter is saved on the device you are using and also synced to the department's online database, so other devices see it after they refresh the page.

The hub: search and Needs attention

Search box Everyone

Type in the search box at the top of the hub to find:

  • Members by name, call sign, rank or phone number. Phone numbers are tap-to-call on a phone.
  • Incidents by incident number, location or type. Choosing one opens the Time Card search with that incident.
  • Addresses for rental and business inspections, or owner names. Choosing one opens Rental Inspections filtered to that address.

Needs attention Everyone

This strip on the hub lists things that are due or overdue, so nothing slips. It covers:

  • Certifications that have expired or expire within 60 days.
  • Rental or business reinspections that are overdue (365 days after the last inspection) or due within 30 days.
  • Vehicle inspections with failed items, and vehicles not inspected in 30 or more days.
  • Truck equipment marked missing.
  • Supply Locker items at or below their reorder level.

Tap a row to jump straight to the place where it can be fixed.

Dashboard Everyone

The Dashboard has four tabs: Call Outs, Roster, Budget & Finance and Rental Inspections. Each gives an at-a-glance summary of that area.

Choosing a period

On Call Outs, use the shortcut buttons (This Month, Last Month, Last 3 Months, Last 12 Months, Year to Date, Last Year, This Fiscal Year, Last Fiscal Year, All Time) or set your own From and To months. The fiscal year runs July 1 to June 30.

Print Summary

Each tab has a 🖨 Print Summary button. A small window lets you tick the sections to include, then your browser's print window opens. Your choices are remembered for next time.

Time Card (Roster & Time Card, Time Card tab)

Logging a call Everyone

Open Timecards and fill in the entry form: incident number (format YY-MFD### or YY-MFR###), date, type, response area, nature of call, dispatch and back-in-service times, location, apparatus, transports, the members who responded and the officer in charge. Fields marked with * are required. Press Add Timecard Entry. One entry creates a time record for each member listed.

Editing or deleting an entry Admin

Opening a saved entry to change or remove it needs an Admin account, because entries touch payroll. Everyone can still view all entries. Deleting asks you to confirm, then shows an Undo button for a few seconds (see "Deleting and Undo").

Special events Admin

Add events that pay a different hourly rate (for example a standby). Retire an event you no longer use instead of deleting it. Delete is only for an event added by mistake.

Pay Roll tab

Choose a date range with the quick buttons (for example Last Month, Last Quarter, This Fiscal Year, Last Fiscal Year) or enter a Start and End date, then press Run Payroll Report. You can filter by position, jump to a person, set a minimum total, or show only people with hours logged.

Search tab Everyone

Search every entry by keyword, incident number, person, entry type, incident type, flags (transported, canceled, missing data), and more. Click a row to open it.

Roster (Roster & Time Card, Roster tab)

The member list

Search and filter members by rank and status (Active, Inactive, Probationary, Leave of Absence). Click a member to open their detail panel with Overview, Certifications, Gear, Contact and Payroll sections.

Adding and editing members Admin

Use Add Member to create a member. Admins also manage each member's login access (their sign-in and role) and pay rate in the member's edit form. Editing and deleting members is hidden from Personnel accounts.

Certifications

Each certification has a completed date and a renewal period (for example CPR and NREMT every 2 years, Fire Fighter 1 and 2 every 3 years). The Roster flags anything expired or coming due in 60 days or less, and the hub's Needs attention strip repeats it.

Reports and printing

Use Due / Overdue, NREMT Roster, Gear Size Report and Fire Fighter Certs for ready-made lists, and Print Roster, Print All Profiles or a single member's Print Profile for paper copies.

On a phone Everyone

Under 900 pixels wide, the Roster becomes a read-only directory showing name, rank, call sign, status and phone number. Tap a number to call. Search by name, call sign, rank or part of a number, and tick Show inactive to include inactive members.

Budget Tracker

Tabs: Expenses, Budget Lines, Vendors and Reports. Pick the fiscal year at the top (July to June).

Logging an expense Everyone

Press Log Expense. Fill in the date, category, description, vendor and amount (required), plus payment method, status, PO or invoice number, who requested it and notes. Press Save Expense. Click any expense to see its details.

Budget lines, grants, editing and deleting Admin

Setting budget allocations, adding or changing Grants entries, editing expenses, and deleting expenses or vendors need an Admin account.

Vendors

Keep a vendor list (name, category, account number, contact, phone, email, address) so expenses can be picked from a list.

Apparatus Inventory

Pick a vehicle at the top: Engine 110, Rescue 110, Ambulance 115, Brush Truck 113 or Brush Truck 114. Three tabs:

  • Compartment Inventory shows a diagram of the truck. Click a compartment to see its items, mark what is present, and change quantities. Everyone
  • Vehicle Inspection is the checklist. Mark each item Pass, Fail or N/A and submit. The date of the last inspection is kept, and failed items appear on the hub's Needs attention strip. Everyone
  • Resupply lists what the truck is short on and ties into the Supply Locker, so stock can be pulled from there. Everyone

Changing the layout Admin

Adding, renaming, repositioning or deleting compartments, bags and kits, editing the inspection checklist, and resetting a vehicle to its defaults all need an Admin account. Removing an item asks you to confirm and then offers Undo.

Supply Locker

Tabs: Lockers, Inventory, Item Database and QR Codes.

  • Lockers shows each station locker, its doors and shelves, and what is on each shelf.
  • Inventory is for counting stock, including scanning a QR code with the phone's camera. Items at or below their reorder level feed the restock list that appears in Apparatus Resupply. Everyone
  • Item Database is the master list of items: name, on hand, reorder level and on order. Adding, editing, deleting and importing from a CSV file are Admin actions.
  • QR Codes builds printable labels. Tick what you want (locker, door, shelf, item, or a vehicle inspection label and its compartments and bags) and print. Truck items do not get labels; each vehicle gets one inspection label.

Structural changes (shelves, bags, kits, sets, renaming, reset) need an Admin account. Deletes ask for confirmation and then offer Undo.

Rental Inspections

Records the Town rental and business fire and safety inspections. Tabs: Dashboard and Inspections.

Logging an inspection Everyone

Press Log Inspection. Choose the type (rental or business), enter the owner, address and date, work through the checklist (Pass, Not Passed or N/A), add notes and who inspected, choose the result, and press Save Inspection.

Reinspections

A property is due for reinspection 365 days after its latest inspection. Open an inspection and press Reinspect to start a new one for the same address. The detail view also lists other inspections at the same address. Overdue and soon-due reinspections show on the hub.

Printing

Use Print in an inspection's detail view for the paper form and results.

Editing and deleting Admin

Editing or deleting a saved inspection needs an Admin account. Deleting offers Undo.

Deleting and Undo

Anything that deletes data first asks you to confirm. After you confirm, a bar appears near the bottom of the screen with an Undo button for about 12 seconds. Press it and the item comes back exactly as it was. Press ✕ to dismiss the bar.

Undo only lasts a few seconds. If you miss it, an Admin can restore from a backup (see Backup & Restore).

Printing

Printing opens your browser's normal print window, so you can print to paper or save as a PDF. Dashboards, the Roster, the Time Card, Budget and Rental all have a Print or Print Summary button. Use Portrait for summaries; the Roster list prints best in Landscape.

Backup & Restore Admin

Open it from the Backup & Restore button on the hub (Admins only).

  • Backup All (.json) downloads a file for every module that has saved data. Do this regularly and keep the files somewhere safe.
  • Download Pages (.html) downloads a copy of the program files themselves. This is the software, not your data.
  • Restore From Backup: drag one or more .json backup files onto the box (or click to choose them) and they are loaded back in. Restoring replaces the current data for those modules.

Troubleshooting

"Only Admin accounts can make this change."

That action is limited to Admins. Ask an Admin to do it for you.

I can't sign in.

Check that you are using your last name and the right password. If it still fails, ask an Admin to reset your password from the Roster.

I don't see someone's change on my screen.

Refresh the page. Changes sync through the online database and appear after a refresh.

The Roster on my phone shows only a list.

That is intended. The phone view is read-only so numbers are quick to find and call. Use a computer or tablet for full details.

I deleted something by mistake.

Press Undo right away if the bar is still showing. Otherwise ask an Admin to restore from a backup.
